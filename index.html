What this is

A private way to notice patterns in how your body is doing. Each check-in records one minute of you talking, then a voice model listens to how you speak (pitch, rhythm, energy, pauses), not what you say. It gives two signals: anxiety and low mood.

It may help with body awareness, including for people with alexithymia who find it hard to name what they feel. Rate yourself before you see the result, then compare over time.

What the research supports

  • In an independent study, the model separated people with and without depression with an accuracy (AUC) of about 0.71, where 0.5 is a coin flip and 1.0 is perfect. Real, but modest.
  • It worked worse for some groups (for example, pregnant women). No independent anxiety accuracy has been published yet.
  • It was trained on mostly American English speech, about 35,000 people. Other languages and accents may be less accurate.
  • Levels line up with two standard questionnaires: PHQ-9 (depression) and GAD-7 (anxiety).
  • Trends over many check-ins matter more than any single reading. Colds, allergies, tiredness, and hormones can all change your voice.

This is a personal tracking and research tool, not a medical device. It can't diagnose anything. Diagnosis and treatment need a doctor or licensed clinician.

Privacy

Your recording is analyzed on this phone and then thrown away. Only the scores, your ratings, and notes are saved, in this app only. Nothing is sent anywhere. The model was downloaded once from this site, and the app checks its fingerprints.
